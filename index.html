About

Design, kept with intention.

Brooklyn Design Archive is a living archive of designed objects. Furniture, lighting, books and ephemera, collected and studied in Brooklyn.

It treats design as culture worth keeping. The collection is lent to exhibitions, written about in Brooklyn Design Review and opened to visitors by appointment.
